3AM_PRODSTEER_TCO_PHIL_SOLAREA - Purpose of Use #1 - 2025-12-05

Purpose

Project Philadelphia – Product Steering (Submission via EAWOCO-1353):

A new product-based company wide steering approach for Cloud Product Cost. Steering of Cloud Business through product cost transparency. Purpose: improvement of the investment decision-making process by getting cost information on product level

  • Dashboard Builder or Analyzer should use this Output Port for providing analytics on Data Products.
  • Data Product Owner, Engineer, Domain Owner, Governance Manager and Operations Teams can use this Output Port for analyzing the current status of their live Data Products as well as their roadmap.
  • Works Council, their Subject Matter Experts and Working Committee can use this Output Port for analyzing Output Ports within their responsibility


Scope of Project:

  • The implementation was carried out in Datasphere.
  • The required dimensions are attached.
  • Reporting uses an SAC Data Analyzer report based on an Analytics Model from Datasphere.
  • The description of the Analytics Model is available in Collibra.
  • Purpose of use: Product controllers use the data to measure the profitability of cloud products.


Submission for approval of the Excel download (Submission via EAWOCO-1377):

1) What is selected for Download and why?
Focus on dimensions such as infrastructure (account) and product location (represented via order and profit center).

2) Who downloads what and where is it stored?
Controlling downloads the data; it is stored on a restricted SharePoint.

3) What does the user do with the list after the download?
Analyses to further break down product costs into detailed components. The SAC report shows the data in aggregated form (for performance and data security reasons). However, there are cases where the aggregated data needs to be mapped to the source system (CBR) via download (Please find this in more detail in the attached PowerPoint file for Download Submission to WOCO). In addition, there is no data forwarding possible to individuals who do not have GCON24 authorization.

4) What does the recipient do with the list and why?
No data forwarding to additional recipients allowed. due to upload on restricted SharePoint.

5) Who has system-level download authorization and approval rights, as well as access to the Data Analyzer?

Only controllers with GCON24 authorization (governed by Group Controlling) have access to the SAC report and, therefore, to the data.

Einreichung zur Genehmigung des Excel-Downloads (Einreichung über EAWOCO-1377): 1) Was wird zum Download ausgewählt und warum? Fokus auf Dimensionen wie Infrastruktur (Konto) und Produktstandort (dargestellt über Auftrags- und Profitcenter). 2) Wer lädt was herunter und wo wird es gespeichert? Controlling lädt die Daten herunter; sie werden auf einem eingeschränkten SharePoint gespeichert. 3) Was macht der Nutzer mit der Liste nach dem Download? Analysen zur weiteren Aufschlüsselung der Produktkosten in detaillierte Komponenten. Der SAC-Bericht zeigt die Daten aggregiert (aus Leistungs- und Datensicherheitsgründen). Es gibt jedoch Fälle, in denen die aggregierten Daten über den Download dem Quellsystem (CBR) zugeordnet werden müssen (siehe hierzu die detaillierte PowerPoint-Datei zur Download-Einreichung an WOCO). Zudem ist keine Datenweitergabe an Personen ohne GCON24-Berechtigung möglich. 4) Was macht der Empfänger mit der Liste und warum? Keine Datenweitergabe an weitere Empfänger erlaubt, aufgrund des Uploads auf den eingeschränkten SharePoint. 5) Wer hat systemweite Download-Berechtigung und Genehmigungsrechte sowie Zugriff auf den Data Analyzer? Nur Controller mit GCON24-Berechtigung (gesteuert durch Group Controlling) haben Zugriff auf den SAC-Bericht und somit auf die Daten.
