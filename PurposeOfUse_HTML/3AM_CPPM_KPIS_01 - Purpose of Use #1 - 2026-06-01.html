3AM_CPPM_KPIS_01 - Purpose of Use #1 - 2026-06-01

Purpose

Analytical model for CPPM (Customer and Partner Performance Management) which sources the data for the KPIs relative to entire CPIT Customer and Partner L2's "End-to-End Demand to Consume Value Stream" encompassing marketing, sales, adoption, and renewals (e.g. Quote Auto Approval %, Invoice Automation Cloud %). These KPIs are organised into the following categories by Customer and Partner vertical: Demand Generation; Deal Execution; Order to Cash; Adopt to Consume; Channels & Cross Engineering Experience & Excellence; BASE; M&A; E2E Business Capabilities; CPIT C&P Compass.


Target Audience: The Data Product is tailored for executives, managers, and analysts within organisations with a focus of enhance customer and Partner process and maximise business outcomes through data-driven insights. 
Beschreibung

Analytisches Modell, das die Daten für die KPIs in Bezug auf den gesamten Kunden- und Partner-"End-to-End Demand to Consume Value Stream"

Zielgruppe: Das Data Product ist auf Führungskräfte, Manager und Analysten in Unternehmen zugeschnitten, mit dem Schwerpunkt, Kunden- und Partnerprozesse zu verbessern und Geschäftsergebnisse durch datengestützte Erkenntnisse zu maximieren.
