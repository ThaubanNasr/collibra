Purpose

Transactional data of Qualtrics surveys, based on Product Experience (PX) Surveys data product extended with Survey to LPR mapping, Solution Areas, Tenants and Customers. This derived data product is only used for the team internal SVB preparation (input for the SVB Factsheet and the SVB briefing documents) of the P&E Business Steering Team. 

Purpose *

This data product would be used for:

  • More automated and reliable PX reporting the SVB preparation of P&E organization as the PX results are one of the 3 main KPIs which are reported
  • Holistic view on root causes of user pain points and customer churn by combining various data sets.
  • Early warning about customers that might not renew contracts
