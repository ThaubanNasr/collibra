3AM_PROMOTION_RATE_ANALYSIS_01 - Purpose of Use #1 - 2026-06-12

Purpose

This output port provides the data required to perform the Promotion Rate Analysis on SAP-internal employee level. It is part of the overall Total Rewards Intelligence Revamp to offer a consistent and cohesive overview on SAP's compensation for all Total Rewards partner (both local and global).

Purpose: Transparency, steering, and control of compensation structures

User Group(s): Total Rewards Partners
Beschreibung

Dieser Ausgabe-Port stellt die Daten bereit, die für die Durchführung der Promotion Rate Analysis auf SAP-internem Mitarbeiterebene erforderlich sind. Er ist Teil des übergeordneten Total Rewards Intelligence Revamp, um einen konsistenten und kohärenten Überblick über die Vergütung bei SAP für alle Total Rewards Partner (sowohl lokal als auch global) zu bieten.

Zweck: Transparenz, Steuerung und Kontrolle von Vergütungsstrukturen

Benutzergruppe(n): Total Rewards Partners
Authorization

Business Roles for Total Rewards are governed by the Total Rewards team centrally - and are based on the existing IPP role. There are two roles for TR, a global role (only small group for central steering) and a local one based on different company codes for the different TR Partners.

Total Rewards role concept: xxxHRCMP__AA  und xxxHRPAD_LTI (either global TR role or local TR role respectively)

In addition, respective DSP role TOTAL_REWARDS_ALL_R
Authorization (German)
