Beschreibung Analytisches Modell, das Einblicke in die C&P AI Cases Pipeline liefert, welches im entsprechenden SAC Dashboard verwendet wird
Authorization

Access to this model is controlled via two ARM roles: The space consumption role (t.YX0EVS:ITP_VA_CONS_C) and the All or Nothing Data Access Control role (t.YX0EVS:ITP_VA_CONS_R). The approval will be reviewed by the data product owners in alignment with C&P management Directives.

The lowest level of granularity is an AI Case (corresponds to a specific jira or Gorefine entry).

Indirect personal data was flagged because the IDs associated to each case can be entered into the source systems (Jira and GoRefine) where it is possible to employee's names, however, no personal information is stored in the model.
