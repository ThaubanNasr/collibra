Description: The HR Ticket Analytics provides information on Ticket volume, End to End ticketing, Ticket Reopening , Processing time SLA, Utilization,Tickets Resolved, Tickets in Progress, Customer Experience etc. This analytical model is the source for all GSD employees to get an instant overview of their global operating model as well as team-specific KPIs.
