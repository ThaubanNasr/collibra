3AM_FDE_TRACKER_01 - Purpose of Use #1 - 2026-04-30

Purpose


This data product offers insights into the status of the Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) activities within SAP. Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) works directly with SAP’s most strategic customers to deliver tailored AI solutions that address high-value business challenges. These solutions are rapidly implemented by Forward Deployed Product Teams and later productized as part of SAP’s standard AI offerings. The outcome is a live AI solution that delivers measurable business value, with the customer serving as a reference as the solution is refined and added to the standard roadmap.

This output port provides a comprehensive view on the overall status of the FDE programm. Following business questions can be answered:

- How many FDE projects do we have along the FDE lifecycle?

- What is the execution status of the FDE projects?

- How many live projects do we already have

- How are the FDE projects are spread across the regions / markets?
Beschreibung

Dieser Ausgabe-Port bietet einen umfassenden Überblick über den Gesamtstatus des FDE-Programms. Folgende Geschäftsfragen können beantwortet werden:

- Wie viele FDE-Projekte haben wir entlang des FDE-Lebenszyklus?

- Was ist der Status der FDE-Projekte?

- Wie viele Live-Projekte haben wir bereits

- Wie verteilen sich die FDE-Projekte auf die Regionen / Märkte?
