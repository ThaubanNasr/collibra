Purpose

This report help users to monitor data quality, identify duplicates or inconsistencies, track changes over time, and ensure compliance with internal standards and external regulations. The purpose of master data reporting is to ensure data consistency, accuracy and reliability across systems. This report provides a comprehensive view of core master data and their attributes used for planning, financial analysis and system alignment. The analytical model does not include any actual or transactional data instead, it serves as a baseline for data validation and governance purposes.

Access to master data reporting is limited to roles that need accurate, consistent, cross-organizational data to do their jobs, e.g Executive Leadership who uses master data to track KPIs, Business Analysts to validate data accuracy, Data Managers / Master Data Managers to maintain data governance, quality, and consistency, Operations Managers to ensure smooth day-to-day processes that rely on accurate core data, Finance Teams to support reporting, audits, and financial accuracy and IT and Data Governance Teams to manage data integration and system alignment.

Employees without reporting responsibilities as well as external users will not get access to our master data.
