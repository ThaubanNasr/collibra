3AM_OFFCYCLE_COMP_JOB_JOINED_FOR_TR_ANALYTICS_03 - Purpose of Use #1 - 2025-11-28

Purpose

This output port covers Offcycle and OnCycle KPIs and their attributes (e.g. Number of Oncycles, Number of Off-cycles, TTC Increase Applied, Avg Increase %, TTC Increase Career Move Flag, On/Off-Cycle Category, Offcycle Change Reasons, etc.) on SAP-internal employee level. It is part of the overall TR Intelligence Revamp to offer a consistent and cohesive overview on SAP's compensation for all Total Rewards partner (both local and global) as well as the P&C leadership (Executive Board).

Purpose: Transparency, steering, and control of compensation structures

User Group(s): Total Rewards

Analytical Activities:

  • Analyze the cost of various On/Off-Cycle Event Reasons
Beschreibung

Dieser Output-Port umfasst Offcycle- und OnCycle-KPIs und deren Attribute (z. B. Anzahl der Oncycles, Anzahl der Off-cycles, TTC Increase Applied, Avg Increase %, TTC Increase Career Move Flag, On/Off-Cycle Category, Offcycle Change Reasons usw.) auf SAP-internem Mitarbeiterebene. Er ist Teil des gesamten TR Intelligence Revamp, um eine konsistente und kohärente Übersicht über die Vergütung von SAP für alle Total Rewards Partner (sowohl lokal als auch global) sowie die P&C Führungsebene (Executive Board) zu bieten.

Zweck: Transparenz, Steuerung und Kontrolle von Vergütungsstrukturen
Benutzergruppe(n): Total Rewards
Analytische Aktivitäten: Analyse der Kosten verschiedener On/Off-Cycle Ereignisgründe
Authorization

Business Roles for Total Rewards are governed by the Total Rewards team centrally - and are based on the existing IPP role. There are two roles for TR, a global role (only small group for central steering) and a local one based on different company codes for the different TR Partners.

Total Rewards role concept: xxxHRCMP__AA  und xxxHRPAD_LTI (either global TR role or local TR role respectively)

In addition, respective DSP role TOTAL_REWARDS_ALL_R
