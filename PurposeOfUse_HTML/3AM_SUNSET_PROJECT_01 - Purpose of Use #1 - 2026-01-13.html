3AM_SUNSET_PROJECT_01 - Purpose of Use #1 - 2026-01-13

Purpose

This Analytical Model provides detailed information about Sunset Projects out of the Project System (cpro). It provides information when a project was created, in which Phase a project is, the deployment mode of the involved product, the related Solution Area and other information that helps the Operations and Product Development Organisations to drive their sunset Projects.

This will be exposed in the Portfolio Sunset Dashboard and will provide a view where the users can check the overview and details to related sunset Projects.

The Sunset Coaches, Sunset Leads, Product Managers will get with this report all relevant information about ongoing Sunset Projects to further prioritize and drive activities related to Product Sunset.

Access to the SAC Dashboard will be provided with a Role which will only be provided to Users from Product Management or in other roles responsible for Managing Sunset Projects.
Beschreibung Dieses Analytische Modell bietet detaillierte Informationen über Sunset Projects aus dem Project System (cpro).
